Where NautIQ pulls ahead

Built different from every other boat app

Most apps track "is it overdue?" NautIQ tracks how fast things are getting worse — and why.

01
Boat Health Score

Risk that compounds — with a plain-language explanation

NautIQ's Health Score is a 0–100 composite that accelerates the further a component is past its service interval and compounds further when linked spares are running low. Every score comes with a "why this score?" breakdown so you're never looking at a number and guessing.

Other apps track a binary: overdue or not. NautIQ tracks the trajectory.

Competitors show overdue · yes/no. NautIQ shows how much worse it's getting.
02
Logging

Chat, email, or camera — your choice, one system

Log a trip by typing naturally, forwarding a marina receipt, or scanning a label with your camera. All three produce reviewable draft cards before anything saves — so you stay in control without the friction of forms.

Most maintenance apps still require manual field entry. NautIQ extracts the data for you, whichever way you prefer to work.

Competitors: fill the form. NautIQ: just tell it what happened.
03
Inventory

Low spares raise the risk score — automatically

When a critical spare runs out, the component it supports gets a higher risk score, which flows up into the overall Boat Health Score. Every competitor treats inventory and maintenance as separate modules. NautIQ is the only app where they're connected.

That linkage means your health score reflects real-world readiness, not just service dates.

Every other app: inventory and maintenance in separate silos.
04
Cost Tracker

Spend by system — not just a running total

See where your money actually goes: a donut chart by category and system, a year-over-year comparison table, and per-trip fuel costs — all at a glance. The level of breakdown you'd normally only get from fleet-management software, included for every boat.

Competitors: expense list. NautIQ: breakdown by system with year-over-year.
